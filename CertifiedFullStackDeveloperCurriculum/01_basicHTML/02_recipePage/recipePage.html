Mocha Sauce

An easy sauce to turn your morning latte into a mocha!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 c unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 c sugar
  • 1/8 tsp kosher salt
  • 3/4 c cold water
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. In a cold saucepan, whisk together the cocoa and the sugar until all lumps are removed.
  2. Add the salt and the water and bring to a boil over medium to medium high heat.
  3. Reduce to a simmer; stirring constantly.
  4. Simmer for about 30 seconds and then remove from heat.
  5. Let cool and then add vanilla.
  6. Pour into a glass jar and refrigerate until needed.
  7. Enjoy!
